===================================================================
STARTINGCORE INTERNSHIP CERTIFICATE
===================================================================

Put your StartingCore internship certificate file in this folder!

Supported file names:
1. internship-certificate.pdf  (Recommended if your certificate is PDF)
2. internship-certificate.png  (or .jpg / .webp if your certificate is an image)

How it works:
- When you drop your certificate file named "internship-certificate.pdf" 
  or "internship-certificate.png" into this folder, clicking 
  "View Certificate" on the StartingCore Experience card on your portfolio 
  will instantly open your certificate in the interactive viewer modal!

- Both PDF and image formats are supported with fullscreen preview, 
  download capability, and open-in-new-tab options.
