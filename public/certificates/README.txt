======================================================
SARTHAK HASE - CERTIFICATES FOLDER
======================================================

Put your certificate files (PDF, PNG, JPG, or WEBP) in this folder!

For example:
1. cert1.pdf (or cert1.png)
2. cert2.pdf (or cert2.png)

Once you place your files here, you can link them in the 
certificates configuration in views/index.ejs:
- file: '/certificates/cert1.pdf' (or .png)
- file: '/certificates/cert2.pdf' (or .png)

Both images (PNG/JPG/WEBP) and PDFs are supported with 
instant fullscreen interactive preview!
